What's protected

Content, original filenames and optional human label are inside encrypted ciphertext. Tampering and wrong passphrases fail authenticated decryption; exact SHA-256 per file and bound lengths are also checked on restore.

What isn't protected

File size, presence of a vault, plaintext after manual restore/download, a weak passphrase, compromised devices, browser extensions or recipients. Cryptographic integrity is not an independent source or authorization check.
